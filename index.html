I'm a research data analyst at the Slone Epidemiology Center, Boston University, where I work on the Black Women's Health Study — analyzing RNA-seq, tumor pathology, and tissue microarray data to understand how metabolic conditions and medication use shape breast cancer biology. I also study polygenic risk scores for ovarian cancer with the National Cancer Institute.

Boston, Massachusetts MSPH Biostatistics, Emory BSc Mathematics, UBC

What I work on

Three questions I keep returning to.

What tumor biology says about exposure

Differential expression, pathway enrichment, and immune deconvolution to connect obesity, type 2 diabetes, and antihypertensive use to gene expression in breast cancer — stratified by ER status and molecular subtype.

Whether genetics sharpens risk prediction

Building and validating polygenic risk score models for ovarian cancer in the PLCO and CPS-II cohorts, testing whether they improve absolute risk estimation beyond established epidemiologic factors.

How to model messy, mixed exposures

Independent methods research combining quantile g-computation with Bayesian additive regression trees, aiming for health impact estimates from environmental mixtures that stay both accurate and interpretable.
